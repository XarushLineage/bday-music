How It Works

Pick a birthday and TopSong looks up the year-end #1 song plus the monthly #1 when monthly Hot 100 data is available.

Year-end results go back to 1946. Entries from 1946 through 1957 use Billboard's pre-Hot-100 retail-sales lists.

Monthly results begin in August 1958, when the Billboard Hot 100 launched. If your date has no monthly entry, the app centers the year result by itself.

Videos use stored YouTube IDs. If an embed cannot load, each card still links directly to YouTube.

Songs are based on Billboard chart history. Built for GitHub Pages.
